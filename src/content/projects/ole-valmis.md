## Overview

Ole Valmis! is a crisis preparedness mobile app designed to help Estonian residents build emergency readiness through an approachable, gamified experience. The project ran from initial research through client-validated prototyping.

## Research

Conducted 10 user interviews and attended a crisis preparedness training session to ground the design in real user mental models and official guidance. Key finding: users understood *that* they needed supplies but not *why* specific items were on the list — leading to distrust and non-compliance.

## The Pivotal Insight

During usability testing, a participant said: "Why do I need a trash bag? I'm not sorting trash if there's a war." This single quote revealed the core problem — the supply list lacked context. Users needed to understand the dual-use logic behind each item (a trash bag doubles as an emergency raincoat or sleeping bag barrier) to trust and act on the recommendations.

## Design Response

Redesigned the supply list feature to surface dual-use explanations for each item. Added gamification elements to make the preparation process feel achievable rather than overwhelming. Iterated from low-fidelity wireframes to high-fidelity interactive prototype across two testing rounds.

## Outcome

The client's development specialist reached out directly for continued collaboration following prototype validation — the strongest signal that the design direction was viable and worth building.
