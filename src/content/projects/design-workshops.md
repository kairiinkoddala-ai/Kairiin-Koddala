## Overview

Conducting engaging design workshops at the Estonian Academy of Arts to teach complex systems and design thinking principles to young people, while developing innovative lecture formats to increase learner engagement.

## Approach

**Interactive Learning** — Hands-on activities that reinforce theoretical concepts through doing rather than passive listening.

**Visual Communication** — Using diagrams and visual aids to explain abstract ideas, making complex systems tangible and memorable.

**Peer Collaboration** — Group exercises that encourage knowledge sharing and learning from each other's perspectives.

**Real-world Applications** — Connecting design principles to everyday experiences so participants can immediately see relevance.

## Impact

- 100+ workshop participants across multiple sessions
- High engagement rates maintained throughout all sessions
- Developed a replicable workshop format adaptable to different topics
- Positive feedback on learning outcomes and practical applicability
