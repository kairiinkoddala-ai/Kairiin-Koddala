## Overview

Designed an intuitive mobile app that helps users correctly sort waste by scanning items, providing instant sorting guidance, and tracking environmental impact to promote sustainable behaviours.

## Research

Studied waste sorting habits and common confusion points through behavioural research. Key findings showed that unclear packaging symbols and municipality-specific rules were the primary barriers to correct sorting.

## Approach

**Behavioural Research** — Studied waste sorting habits and common confusion points to understand where the process breaks down.

**Computer Vision** — Implemented AI recognition for instant item identification, removing the need to manually search through sorting guides.

**Localisation** — Adapted sorting rules for different Estonian municipalities, since rules vary significantly by region.

**Gamification** — Added an achievement system to encourage consistent use and build long-term sorting habits.

## Results

- 40% improvement in sorting accuracy
- 25,000+ scans completed
- 85% user confidence increase
- Coverage of 500+ common household items
