problem: "How do you make the invisible electrical life of plants audible — and in doing so, remind visitors of their own material kinship with the living world? The initial technical hypothesis (external MIDI hardware integration) proved clinically disconnected from the biophilic intent of the work, introducing prohibitive latency and a visual tech-centeredness antithetical to the installation's meditative purpose."
problemEt: "Kuidas muuta taimede nähtamatu elektriline elu kuuldavaks — ja seeläbi meelde tuletada külastajatele nende enda materiaalset sugulust elava maailmaga? Esialgne tehniline hüpotees (väline MIDI riistvara integratsioon) osutus biofiiilse kavatsuse suhtes kliiniliselt eraldatuks."
solution: "A Suunamuutus — a directional pivot — to a fully browser-based Web Audio API architecture. Bio-electric signals from living plant tissue are captured via ADS1115 high-accuracy converters, smoothed through a rolling average algorithm, and mapped to Tintinnabuli-inspired tonal parameters in real time. The physical installation is a sealed greenhouse enclosure built from raw timber and translucent diffuser canvas, housing living plants and soil that visitors touch directly to complete the bio-electric circuit."
solutionEt: "Suunamuutus — suunamuutus — täielikult brauseripõhisele Web Audio API arhitektuurile. Elavate taimede bioelektrilisi signaale kogutakse ADS1115 kõrgtäpsuslike muundurite kaudu, silutakse libiseva keskmise algoritmiga ja kaardistakse reaalajas Tintinnabuli-inspireeritud tonaalsetele parameetritele."
outcome: "A completed exhibition installation presented at EKA in 2025. The work demonstrates that browser-based bio-electric sound synthesis — without proprietary hardware — is viable, affordable, and conceptually coherent with biophilic spatial design. Visitor interactions confirmed the meditative quality of the encounter: the boundary between observer and organism dissolved through touch and sound."
outcomeEt: "Lõpetatud näituse installatsioon, mis esitleti EKAs 2025. aastal. Teos näitab, et brauseripõhine bioelektriline helisüntees — ilma patenteeritud riistvarata — on teostatav, taskukohane ja kontseptuaalselt kooskõlas biofiiilse ruumidisainiga."
