## Overview

As a Service Design intern at the Estonian Tax and Customs Board (MTA), I focused on two parallel workstreams: standardising how development processes are documented across product teams, and creating visual communication materials that make service design principles accessible to technical audiences.

## Approach

**Information Architecture** — Restructured the Wiki documentation hierarchy to enable intuitive navigation. The previous structure had grown organically over time, making it difficult for new team members and external partners to find relevant information quickly.

**Visual Communication** — Created diagrams and visual guides to explain abstract service design concepts. The 14-page guide bridges the gap between design thinking and technical implementation, giving developers a shared language for discussing service quality.

**Process Standardisation** — Developed templates and frameworks so all product teams document their processes consistently. This reduces onboarding time and makes cross-team collaboration significantly smoother.

**Stakeholder Collaboration** — Worked directly with developers and external partners throughout the process to validate that the new structures were practical and usable in real workflows, not just theoretically sound.

## Results

- All MTA product teams now use the standardised documentation framework
- +0.1 improvement in Google rating
- Increased web traffic via improved Search Console performance
- 14-page comprehensive visual guide created and distributed
