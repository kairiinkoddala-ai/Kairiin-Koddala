## Overview

Botik Garden Bar is a Tallinn venue with a strong atmospheric identity. I own the full content and growth strategy — no external agency, no brief handed down. Built and executed everything from data analysis to publishing.

## Strategy

Developed a low-profile, exclusivity-first content approach aligned with the owner's brand vision. The strategy deliberately avoids promotional language, instead using consistent atmospheric storytelling to build desire and drive inbound inquiries.

## Operations Work

Beyond content, identified and fixed recurring operational friction points:
- Built an inventory feedback loop with bartenders to flag stock issues in real time
- Rebuilt front-of-house workflow before peak season to reduce bottlenecks during high-capacity nights
- Initiated quarterly analytics overviews for ownership, translating platform data into strategic decisions

## Results

- **+27.3%** inbound inquiry volume
- **+98.7%** social traffic retention
- Google average position improved from **9.5 to 6.2** — a **29.8% increase** in organic search traffic
