## The Team

Independent / Solo Execution

## The Problem

The venue's founder, Juula, approached me because Botik's social media channels had been dormant for a long time. Locals were completely unsure if the venue was even operating anymore.

While I started purely as a social media manager, I quickly noticed deeper systemic friction. Because Botik originally launched primarily as a local bar, their entire website structure, search engine indexing, and historical reviews reflected that single identity. They weren't surfacing anywhere online as a premium event rental venue for companies. I stepped up to overhaul their entire digital footprint.

## The Solution

I treated Botik's digital ecosystem like a living product funnel, optimizing it from first click to physical visit.

- **Information Architecture Overhaul** — I rebuilt the website from scratch — purging dead pages, writing clean, SEO-friendly copywriting, and simplifying complex navigation menu items into plain language.
- **B2B Funnel Creation** — Since corporate clients have entirely different needs than casual bar guests, I designed a dedicated B2B landing page on the site. To bridge the digital-to-physical gap, I built a physical B2B sales booklet featuring room blueprints I drew myself to make event planning completely seamless.
- **Ecosystem Directory Cleanup** — I cleaned up external directories, working directly with platforms like Visit Estonia to correct out-of-date venue listings and push our event space capabilities forward.

## Designing Around Zero Budget

As a lovely family business, Botik operates with a near-zero marketing budget. This forced me to master organic growth loops. I drove top-of-funnel traffic entirely through community distribution — negotiating collaborative trades with local photographers (giving them free access to our beautiful space in exchange for high-quality content rights) and seeding free, highly-targeted posts across local Facebook interest groups. This scrappy approach earned immense creative trust from the owners and allowed me to treat the venue as a real-world product lab.

## The Pivot

I stopped guessing and turned to data. By deep-diving into Google Analytics, I discovered exactly where users were dropping out of our digital booking funnel. I completely re-packaged the navigation names for our menus and built a standalone landing page targeted directly to corporate needs. This operational pivot pushed our organic search rank from 9.5 down to 6.2 and secured a 27.3% surge in high-value inbound business inquiries.

## Results

- **+27.3%** inbound inquiry volume
- **+98.7%** social traffic retention
- Google average position improved from **9.5 to 6.2** — a **29.8% increase** in organic search traffic
